- First release: an extra, auto-hiding launcher on the display and edge (left, right or bottom) you choose.
- Shows the apps pinned in your Dock, then other open apps, and follows changes to your Dock.
- Slides out when the pointer reaches the edge; on an edge another display touches, a short pause first.
- With Accessibility: a window list in the right-click menu, click the app in front to minimize it, and Apple's Dock kept away from EchoEdge's edge.
- Shows over full-screen apps (can be turned off).
